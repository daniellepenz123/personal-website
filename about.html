About Danielle

Experience, perspective, and purpose.

I’m Danielle Penz—a survivor leader, advocate, public speaker, and developer working across anti-trafficking advocacy, education, and technology.

My perspective

Lived experience informs my work.

My lived experience showed me that exploitation does not always resemble the version of trafficking the public is taught to recognize. That understanding shapes the questions I ask and the work I pursue.

My focus includes human trafficking, coercive control, technology-facilitated exploitation, and the challenges survivors encounter when seeking recognition, support, and opportunity.

Through advocacy and education, I bring attention to experiences that are often overlooked and encourage more thoughtful responses to exploitation. I value clear communication, careful questions, and a willingness to examine assumptions.

Survivor leadership

Expertise that should shape decisions.

I believe lived experience should be recognized as expertise. Survivors bring knowledge that can inform research, education, policy, technology, and program development.

I advocate for meaningful, compensated opportunities for survivors to participate in leadership and decision-making. Their involvement should include influence over the work, respect for their boundaries, and recognition of their professional contributions.

Technology & professional development

Connecting technical skills with purpose.

Technology is a connected part of my professional direction. I work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript while expanding my knowledge of cybersecurity, digital investigations, and digital forensics.

I’m particularly interested in how these fields can help address digital coercion, strengthen survivor safety, and improve understanding of technology-facilitated exploitation.

My continued education also includes survivor leadership, victim advocacy, public speaking, and anti-trafficking work. I’m building a professional path that brings these areas together.

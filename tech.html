Technology, coercion & investigation

Technical knowledge. Survivor-informed purpose.

I build websites and create education about digital coercion. My continued technical development focuses on cybersecurity, digital investigations, and digital forensics.

The connection

Why technology is part of my advocacy.

I examine how digital systems enable coercion, how evidence is understood in context, and how technical tools can support survivor safety.

01 / Digital coercion

Understanding technology-facilitated control.

I educate others about digital coercion: using technology to monitor, pressure, threaten, or control someone through accounts, communication, or private information.

The focus is consent, control over access, privacy, and autonomy.

02 / Cybersecurity & privacy

Developing cybersecurity knowledge with survivor safety in mind.

I’m expanding my knowledge of cybersecurity, digital privacy, and ethical security testing. My learning focuses on protecting accounts, devices, and sensitive information.

I study how security practices address coercion, shared access, and technology- facilitated abuse.

03 / Digital investigations & forensics

Developing an investigative foundation.

I study open-source intelligence (OSINT)—evaluating publicly available information—and digital forensics, the examination of data from devices and digital systems.

I’m developing skills in assessing information, identifying patterns, and interpreting digital evidence, guided by accuracy, privacy, and ethical practice.

04 / Software development

Practical skills for building solutions.

I build responsive websites with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, including forms and interactive features.

My development work emphasizes clear navigation, accessibility, and usability.

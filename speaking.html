Speaking & education

Perspective that deepens understanding.

I bring lived experience and a survivor-informed perspective to conversations about human trafficking, coercive control, digital safety, and leadership.

My approach

Clear communication. Thoughtful discussion.

My approach is direct, thoughtful, and grounded in respect for the complexity of survivors’ experiences. I make space for questions while challenging assumptions that can limit how exploitation is understood.

I aim to make these conversations accessible to people with different levels of familiarity, while preserving the language and context needed to discuss them accurately.

My focus is on connecting awareness with reflection: what may be overlooked, how responses can improve, and where survivor expertise belongs in that process.

Areas of focus

Speaking topics.

Human trafficking and misconceptions about exploitation

Examining the assumptions that shape public understanding of trafficking and the experiences those assumptions can leave out.

Coercive control and barriers to recognition

Considering patterns of control, the context of exploitation, and why a person’s circumstances may be misunderstood.

Survivor identification and trauma-informed responses

Exploring recognition and response through a survivor-informed perspective, with attention to autonomy, context, and professional practice.

Technology-facilitated exploitation and digital coercion

Connecting the use of technology for surveillance, threats, and control with broader conversations about exploitation.

Digital safety and survivor privacy

Considering privacy and digital safety in the context of survivors’ circumstances, priorities, and access to technology.

Survivor leadership and meaningful professional inclusion

Examining what meaningful participation looks like, including compensation, professional boundaries, and influence over decisions.

Gaps in systems and opportunities for change

Discussing how survivor expertise can inform research, education, services, and the decisions that shape anti-trafficking work.
